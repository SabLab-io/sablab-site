Idelio

AI concierge for hotels A Sablab product

Idelio answers a hotel's guests by phone and on WhatsApp, in Spanish and English. It answers questions using the hotel's own information, and it takes bookings and service requests for the hotel team.

  • Phone calls

    A voice agent answers guests' calls to the hotel.

  • WhatsApp

    Guests send a WhatsApp message and get an answer.

  • Spanish and English

    Idelio replies in the language the guest uses.

  • The hotel's own information

    Answers come from what the hotel provides, such as rooms, hours and services.

  • Bookings and requests

    Idelio takes bookings and service requests and passes them to staff.

  • Admin dashboard

    Hotel staff set up and manage Idelio from a web dashboard.
